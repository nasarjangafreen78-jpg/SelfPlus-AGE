What Is Resveratrol, and Why Do People Take It?

Resveratrol is a polyphenol, a type of plant compound. It has been studied for more than 20 years. The company's website points to a 2024 systematic review that identified 194 human clinical studies, covering topics like oxidative stress markers, glucose and insulin, and cardiovascular and cellular health.

Here is the honest part: "studied" does not mean "proven." Human trials on resveratrol have produced mixed results. Many are small, doses and durations vary a lot, and the body does not absorb resveratrol very efficiently. So resveratrol should not be treated as a miracle ingredient. If a website promises dramatic results, be skeptical.
